## Local exceptions

EyeOff keeps dash coefficients `[0, 2]`, a unit-length gap and offset `[0, -2]`. Fingerprint keeps six dash values, interval easeOut and its computed dash-ratio replay condition. Its opacity has five values and an independent duration. ReceiptText starts from the current full dash, hides, holds a plateau and reveals with explicit offsets and linear timing. UserRoundPlus returns opacity only; its interrupted dash remains until cleanup. AtSign measures circle and path separately and keeps the outer 300 ms delay on return. FolderTree and Route preserve their actual delays in both directions. GitCompareArrows keeps its delayed opacity-only returns, and X keeps its second 200 ms return delay. BluetoothConnected preserves opposite offset signs and its separate opacity waveform.

WindArrowDown cannot be migrated as a complete recipe by this proposed paint adapter. Its final paths call the sampled SVG transform helper with a retained Y pose and opacity, a 350 ms entry delay and until-clear replay. A complete path-plus-translation adapter requires another representation decision and independent interruption evidence. Keep its current Motion definition intact. This Phase 1 blocker belongs to #103 Phase 2; a concrete linked rollout follow-up is still required if it remains blocked after architecture review.

The other 107 capability candidates and all 242 Motion retentions preserve their decisions and hashes. There is no production dependency change to renew in this proposal. No spring, queued phase or retained velocity was simplified.
